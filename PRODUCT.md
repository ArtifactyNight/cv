# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Kidsanaphon Kaeopha for engineering roles. They arrive with limited time, often on a laptop between meetings, scanning for credibility, scope of work, and a reason to reach out. The site must answer who you are, what you build, and how to contact you without hunting.

## Product Purpose

A personal brand surface that helps land interviews and offers. Success is a clear hire signal: the visitor understands your level, taste, and fit, then takes the next step (email, LinkedIn, or scheduling). Secondary routes (Thoughts, Playlists, Contact) support personality and depth but must not obscure the primary recruiter path on About.

## Brand Personality

Playful, crafted, memorable. Voice is confident and human, not corporate. Playfulness shows up in intentional details (motion, typography rhythm, small delights), not novelty for its own sake. Craft means the implementation quality is part of the portfolio proof.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing page patterns: hero metrics, identical feature card grids, buzzword copy, gradient accents as decoration
- Generic AI portfolio tells: cream or sand body backgrounds, gradient text, an uppercase eyebrow above every section, numbered section markers used as default scaffolding
- Resume-as-PDF on the web: dense bullet walls with no hierarchy or scan path

Reference direction (borrow, do not copy): minimal dev portfolio sites with fast scan, clear hierarchy, and little ornament.

## Design Principles

1. **Recruiter scan path first.** About and Contact must communicate role, location, and reachability within seconds; deeper pages reward curiosity but never block the hire signal.
2. **Playfulness earns its moments.** One memorable interaction beats five identical entrance animations; motion and detail should feel chosen, not templated.
3. **Minimal chrome, maximal signal.** Prefer type, spacing, and hierarchy over cards, badges, and decorative UI. If an element does not help hire or trust, cut it.
4. **Craft is the proof.** Typography, contrast, responsive behavior, and polish are part of the work sample; ship states that look finished, not scaffolded.
5. **Reject marketing grammar.** No SaaS cadence, no aphoristic punch lines, no buzzwords; say what you did with specific nouns and verbs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline accessibility: semantic structure, visible focus, sufficient contrast for body text, keyboard-accessible navigation, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for any animation. No formal WCAG certification target beyond sensible defaults.
