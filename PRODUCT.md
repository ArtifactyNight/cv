# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Peers and builders: developers, designers, and founders who already care about craft. They arrive from a link, a post, or a search result, often on a laptop or phone between tasks. The job is to quickly understand who you are, what you build, and whether your thinking is worth following. They should leave with a clear mental model of your work and an easy path to your writing or contact.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and blog that earns credibility through clarity, not volume. The site introduces you, surfaces selected work, and indexes your writing in a scannable list. Success for v1 is a strong home/profile page that feels unmistakably yours within the first scroll: precise copy, intentional structure, no template noise. The blog supports the same voice but ships after the home surface is right.

## Brand Personality

Precise · quiet · opinionated.

Voice is first-person and measured. You state what you believe and what you build without marketing cadence or performance. The emotional goal is respect and curiosity: peers should think "this person has taste and follows through," not "this is another portfolio."

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing page grammar: hero metrics, identical feature cards, gradient CTAs, buzzword copy.
- Dark-mode dev portfolio cliché: neon accents, monospace as costume, terminal aesthetics without purpose.
- [designerdada.com](https://www.designerdada.com/) is a structural reference (intro → work → writing), not a visual clone. Do not reproduce its exact typography or layout treatment wholesale.

## Design Principles

1. **Structure carries the story.** Lead with intro, then work, then writing. Navigation is implicit in scroll order, not a component zoo.
2. **Quiet is a choice, not an absence.** Sparse layout still needs hierarchy, contrast, and deliberate pacing. Empty is not minimal.
3. **Opinionated over comprehensive.** Curate projects and essays. Resume-dump completeness works against peer trust.
4. **Peer-to-peer, not applicant-to-recruiter.** Write for people who already understand the craft. Skip explainers and credential padding.
5. **Practice what you preach.** The stack (Astro, content collections, performance) should be legible in how the site behaves, not just listed on an about page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline accessibility for v1: semantic HTML, readable contrast on body text, keyboard-reachable links, meaningful alt text when images ship. WCAG AA and reduced-motion polish are deferred but should not be designed out. Thai and English content must render correctly with the committed font pairing (Satoshi + IBM Plex Sans Thai).
